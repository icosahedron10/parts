ABLATION DISCIPLINE

- First inventory each addition: lines/branches it owns, trigger frequency, input/output tokens, latency, changed decisions, and demonstrated benefits or failures. Separate runtime logic and model calls from evaluation-only reporting infrastructure.
- Decouple features before comparing them. In this code, strict=False changes several things at once; it is not an audit-only ablation. The planning flag also controls a group-cycling guard; switching it off would otherwise confound the planner comparison. Inspect other flag interactions.
- Preserve an unchanged control and freeze the case list, source snapshots, prompts, settings, grading rubric and code hashes for each experiment. Change one behavior at a time initially, then check interactions among promising simplifications.
- Use exact-request replays for cheap controlled screening, clearly marked as replays. Never reuse a response after changing its prompt, schema, inputs or model. Confirm the strongest variants and the control with fresh model calls; repeat close or inconsistent outcomes before attributing a difference to the change.
- Do not count reference-number presence as semantic accuracy. Review requested component identity, assembly scope, qualifiers, extra candidates, missed valid candidates and unsupported assertions. Report exact reference match, reference recovery and semantic review separately. Keep failures and abstentions in the denominator.
- Include false negatives and unnecessary clarifications, not just wrong answers. Returning an unconfirmed basket of parts for every question is not success.
